   lineage, all six predecessor/stage IDs, versioned hashes, subjects and
   historical times. Reject extras, duplicate fields and coercion. Measure the

   30-second freshness, 16-owner, 256-global and 256 MiB main-SQLite bounds.
5. **Durability and uncertainty:** select exact domains and permanent

6. **No authority promotion:** consistent declarations with missing runtime or
   contact proof, forged lineage, stale/foreign statuses, invented fields and
   forbidden payloads must remain non-authorizing. Recording cannot clear a
   blocker or create readiness.
