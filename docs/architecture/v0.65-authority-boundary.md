# v0.65 P0 — controlled activation authorization boundary analysis

Status: **Deferred. No distinct, trusted, reference-only control-plane
activation authorization decision is supportable from the tagged repository.**
This is a versioned architecture analysis, not a contract selection or an
implementation plan. It was performed against exact Git baseline
`5790b8eeb6e731271ea88531ec29c877dd0da31d` (the released v0.64 repository
state) on 2026-09-23. No runtime, effect-plane, Agent, worker, deployment,
release, or enablement authority follows.

## Decision

Defer a successor activation-authorization stage. The smallest plausible
successor is a separately owned, reference-only control-plane decision that
would authorize one exact activation subject for one effect-plane consumer;
however, the tagged repository does not prove its trusted author, approval
relationship, authenticated contact, distinct consumer decision, or recovery
semantics. The v0.64 runtime definition is therefore retained evidence, not
that decision.

This conclusion does not select the provisional v0.64 P0 design. It evaluates
its candidate shape against the integrated v0.64 freeze and the older contracts
that would be adjacent consumers.

## Minimum candidate decision, if reopened

This is a test for a future normative contract, not a reserved schema.

### Trusted author and immutable inputs

The contract must name one trusted authority that is independent of the
presentation reader and distinguish it from the requesting operator. It must
bind an authenticated operator/approver, candidate or installation subject,
one exact v0.64 definition fingerprint, artifact digest, runtime identity,
capability set, resource limits, filesystem/mount policy, network policy,
policy-version fingerprint, and the intended effect-plane operation. Inputs
must be closed, canonical, immutable, and reject mutable tags, ambient host
configuration, caller-supplied credentials, latest-record substitution,
unknown fields, coercion, or policy widening.

The trusted author must be explicit about whether Core owns the decision,
whether a separate installation authority signs it, and how that authority is
authenticated. Operator authentication on the v0.64 evidence route is not
proof that the submitted definition is an approved activation decision.

### Distinct consumer decision and ownership

The decision must be consumed by exactly one separately named control-plane
admission component that can distinguish accepted, refused, expired, canceled,
superseded, and indeterminate outcomes. A Mission Control projection, Agent
workflow, queue observation, health check, or worker ledger is not such a
consumer. The owner must be accountable for the decision, while the
effect-plane adapter separately owns execution and reports only a bounded
outcome; neither side may infer authority from the other side's status.

### Approval, expiry, cancellation, and subject identity

Approval must be exact and explicit: who approved, which immutable subject,
which policy version, which artifact and definition fingerprints, the allowed
operation, and the approval time. It must define authenticated contact
requirements for both the approver and the effect-plane peer. Expiry must be
trusted-time and terminal; cancellation and supersession must prevent use
before contact and must not mutate a prior terminal record. Ownership transfer
must be prohibited or require a new decision; no foreign-owner or nested
caller-supplied subject is admissible.

### One-shot uncertainty and recovery

Reservation must be durable and occur before any external effect. A stable
activation ID and idempotency key must make retries converge to one terminal
decision. Concurrency, restart, timeout after contact, adapter loss, partial
completion, corrupt storage, cancellation race, and expiry race must all fail
closed. `indeterminate` must mean “do not retry blindly”; recovery must use
authenticated readback or an explicitly terminal refusal, never replay an
unknown start. No success record may be written after an uncertain effect
without a proof that the exact subject and operation were observed.

## Precise open gates

All gates are open; failure of any one keeps the candidate deferred.

1. **Baseline and lineage:** pass independent Core, Agent, worker, Mission
   Control, and release obligations at the exact predecessor baseline. A
   focused test pass does not close release acceptance.
2. **Trusted provenance:** identify the authoritative writer, authentication
   boundary, policy source, canonical subject, refusal rules, and immutable
   approval inputs. Show conflicting/forged/foreign declarations fail closed.
3. **Distinct decision proof:** demonstrate a concrete consumer decision that
   differs from v0.64 evidence status, including exact positive, refusal,
   expiry, cancellation, supersession, and stale/foreign subject vectors.
4. **Approval/contact relationship:** prove the approver's authority and the
   effect-plane peer's authenticated identity, operation vocabulary, credential
   boundary, and allowed-effect limits. No queue, database, HTTP reachability,
   UI session, or worker attestation substitutes for this proof.
5. **Immutable measurable limits:** measure and freeze envelope/model/storage/
   response bounds; artifact, runtime, capabilities, CPU/memory/PID,
   filesystem/mount, environment, DNS/ports, and policy fingerprints. Reject
   truncation, unknown fields, duplicate keys, mutable references, and
   inherited ambient policy.
6. **One-shot durability:** prove reservation-before-effect, idempotency,
   concurrency, restart/no-replay, corruption detection, terminal expiry and
   cancellation, redacted failure behavior, and bounded authenticated
   readback after uncertain contact.
7. **Isolation and consumer closure:** prove zero Agent, execution-worker,
   operational, queue, deployment, and effect-plane consumers until a later
   decision explicitly selects them. Mission Control remains nested GET-only.
